First up, we have Chrome DevTools.
    - built-in tool inside Google Chrome that lets you analyze and debug performance in real-time
    - show loading times, CPU usage, and render delays
    - useful for measuring First Contentful Paint, or FCP

Next, Lighthouse.
    - automated tool that checks performance, SEO, and accessibility
    - simply open Chrome DevTools and go to the "Lighthouse" tab
    - click "Analyze page load" and it will give you a score on how fast and optimized your site is, 
        also recommendations to improve it
    - If you want a quick performance audit, Lighthouse is your go-to tool.

Now, let's talk about WebPageTest.
    - test how your site loads from different locations and devices
    - visit WebPageTest.org, enter your website URL, choose a test location and browser and click "Start 
        Test"
    - gives a detailed breakdown of your site's Speed Index, Total Blocking Time, and other key 
        performance metrics

Another great tool is Google PageSpeed Insights.
    - analyzes your website and suggests quick improvements for both mobile and desktop
    - go to PageSpeed Insights, enter your URL, and click "Analyze"
    - tell you what's slowing your site down and give specific recommendations — like optimizing images, r
        emoving render-blocking scripts, and reducing server response times.

Real User Monitoring, or RUM tools
    - track actual user behavior, showing how real visitors experience your site
    - include Google Analytics, which tracks page load times and bounce rates

New Relic or Datadog, 
    - which Monitors real-time performance issues.
