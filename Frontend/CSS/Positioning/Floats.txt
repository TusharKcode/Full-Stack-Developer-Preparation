"Floats" in CSS are a technique originally designed to allow text to wrap around an element, 
    such as an image. 
    - When an element is floated, it's taken out of the normal document flow and pushed to the left 
        or right of its container.

    - Floats were also popular for creating multi-column layouts before Flexbox and Grid became 
        widespread.

    - clear: both ensures the pseudo-element clears both sides of any floated elements above it.
